How To Use This System

Follow these 3 simple steps to simulate client creditworthiness and explore neural predictions.

01

Choose or Tune a Profile

Click one of the Scenario Presets (Low-Risk, Borderline, or Delinquent) below, or use the interactive sliders and dropdowns to build a custom customer profile.

02

Inspect Financial Behaviour

Adjust repayment timeliness from April to September (`PAY_0` to `PAY_6`). Values above 0 indicate months of payment delay, heavily influencing default probability.

03

Get Live Risk Diagnosis

The neural network evaluates inputs in real-time (250ms debounce). View the Risk Meter Gauge, probability percentage, and classification decision instantly.
